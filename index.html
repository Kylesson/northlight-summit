Questions

Are the talks in English or Russian?

All talks are in English. Slides and the programme are in English and Russian, and speakers take questions in both.

Can I get a refund?

Yes, a full refund until 17 March 2027. After that you can transfer your ticket instead.

Can I give my ticket to a colleague?

Yes. Send us their name and email up to the day before the event, free of charge.

Is the venue accessible?

The hall is on one level with step-free entry, accessible toilets and a quiet room. Tell us in the form if you need anything else.

Will talks be recorded?

Yes. Every ticket includes the recordings, sent by email about two weeks after the event. Workshops are not recorded.

Can my company get an invoice?

Yes. Add your company details after registering and we send a VAT invoice within two working days.
